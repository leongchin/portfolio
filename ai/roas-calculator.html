Formula Reference — How These Numbers Are Derived
Breakeven ROAS
1 ÷ Gross Margin %
The minimum ROAS at which ad spend = gross profit. Below this, every sale loses money after costs. Directly usable as the ROAS floor in Meta Ads Manager.
Target ROAS
1 ÷ (Gross Margin % − Desired Margin %)
The ROAS required to retain your desired net margin after ad spend. Set this as your campaign target ROAS in Meta or Google Ads.
Gross Profit Margin
(Price − All Costs) ÷ Price
All non-ad costs: COGS, fulfillment, CC fees, merchant fees. This is the gross margin available to absorb ad spend and generate profit.
Max Ad Spend / Sale
Gross Profit − Desired Profit $
The maximum you can pay to acquire one sale (your CPA ceiling) while still hitting your margin target. Use this as your target CPA in campaigns.
